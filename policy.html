Our company’s services are governed by an in-house developed proprietary repair method that we have named: The AAA Advanced® Repair Process.

It includes select industry-standard guides by IPC, IEEE and others, as well as techniques and skills acquired through years of both Academic and Industrial experience.

The process is comprised of three main steps:

Advanced® Initial Testing & Diagnostics

AIR Group goes beyond basic fault finding and board replacement. We perform advanced diagnostics, testing and component-level analysis to identify failed or degraded components and determine the underlying cause of equipment failure.

Our capabilities include the diagnosis and repair of complex electronic assemblies, power electronics, inverter and drive circuitry, control boards, and associated industrial electronics. By working at a component level, we can often provide a more targeted repair rather than simply replacing an entire electronic assembly.

Where appropriate, equipment is subjected to detailed testing following repair to help verify functionality and reliability before it is returned to service.

Advanced® Quality-Focused and Industry-Standard Repairs

Quality and workmanship are the foundation of the way we operate. Our repair processes are carried out with reference to recognized electronics workmanship standards, including IPC-7711/7721, and IPC-A-610, as applicable to the equipment and repair being performed.

We combine these standards with years of practical electronics experience, time-tested repair procedures, careful inspection, and comprehensive testing. Our objective is not simply to make a failed device work again, but to provide a repair that is technically sound and suitable for its intended application.

Advanced® Final In Depth Testing & Verification

Following repair, our equipment undergoes detailed electrical and functional testing to verify that the system is operating correctly across its intended operating parameters. Depending on the equipment, this can include testing and monitoring output voltages, currents, frequencies, control signals, protection circuits, feedback systems, and other critical output parameters. Where practical, we test equipment under representative operating conditions to confirm stable and consistent performance. Our aim is not simply to confirm that a unit powers up, but to thoroughly verify that its outputs, controls, protections, and associated functions are operating as intended before the equipment is returned to service.

Sustainable Development Policy

We believe responsible electronics repair is not only about restoring equipment — it is also about extending the useful life of electronic products and reducing unnecessary waste.

Whenever technically and commercially practical, we aim to use environmentally preferable materials and processes in our repairs. We also strive to work toward RoHS-compliant repair practices, selecting components and materials that meet applicable restrictions on hazardous substances wherever commercially suitable and technically compatible alternatives are available.

Repairing equipment rather than automatically replacing complete assemblies can help reduce electronic waste, conserve resources, and extend the service life of valuable industrial equipment. We are committed to continually improving our processes and sourcing practices to optimize our environmental impact while maintaining the quality and reliability expected from our clients.
