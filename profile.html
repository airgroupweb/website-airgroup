Company Profile: Keeping The Industry Moving

At AIR Group, we specialize in the advanced repair, testing, and diagnostics of inverters, drives, and select commercial and industrial electronic equipment. Our goal is to help businesses get critical equipment back into service reliably, efficiently, and cost-effectively. From a failed inverter controlling a pump to a drive used in an elevator, crane, HVAC system, or manufacturing process, industrial electronics can be critical to keeping equipment operational.

At AIR Group, we believe that repair should be a first-class alternative to replacement when it is technically and economically viable. Our work helps customers reduce unnecessary equipment replacement, minimize downtime, and extend the useful life of valuable industrial electronics. Whether you require repair of a known fault, advanced troubleshooting of an intermittent failure, or component-level diagnostics on a complex electronic assembly, AIR Group is focused on providing a professional, technically driven repair service you can rely on.

SERVICES

Industrial electronic systems are often at the heart of essential machinery. When a drive, inverter, control board, or other electronic assembly fails, replacing the entire system can be expensive, time-consuming, and sometimes not needed at all. Our approach focuses on identifying the actual cause of the fault and, where practical, repairing the equipment at the component level. We support both modern and legacy systems, covering and large variety of inverter brands and models.

Our repaired equipment is used across a wide range of industries and applications, including but not limited to:

  • Elevators and Lifts
  • Escalators
  • Hydraulic systems
  • Pumps
  • Compressors
  • Cranes
  • Fans & HVAC systems
  • Power supplies
  • Other commercial and industrial machinery and electronic devices

We understand that every hour of downtime can have a significant operational and financial impact, which is why we take a methodical and engineering-focused approach to every repair.

Supply & Service Exchange Units

Where a repair is not practical within the required timeframe, or where minimizing equipment downtime is a priority, AIR Group can also provide supply and service exchange units where available. This can provide customers with a faster route to getting critical equipment back into operation while their original unit is assessed, repaired, or serviced. Our exchange options are intended to provide a practical alternative to purchasing new equipment, helping reduce downtime and control replacement costs.

Warranty & Extended Warranty Options

We believe that quality repairs should come with confidence. That is why we offer a comprehensive warranty coverage on eligible repairs, together with extended warranty options available in order to provide additional peace of mind. Our commitment is to deliver reliable repair solutions and dependable support to help our customers protect their equipment, manage maintenance costs, and maintain operational continuity.

Warranty coverage, duration, eligibility, and exclusions are subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

Research & Development

Driving Innovation in Industrial & Commercial Electronics Repair

At our company, we believe that continuous innovation is essential to delivering reliable, efficient, and cost-effective repair solutions. Part of the company’s future roadmap is Investing in research and development (R&D) to further advance our technical capabilities in the repair, testing, and diagnostics of commercial and industrial electronic equipment. Our goal is to develop advanced test systems, diagnostic tools, and innovative repair methods that would help us tackle complex repairs, reduce downtime, and extend equipment service life.

We aim to transform our repair experience into engineering knowledge, engineering knowledge into innovative solutions, and innovative solutions into greater reliability for the industry.

Global Repair Coverage

We provide specialist commercial/industrial repair services to customers worldwide, supporting businesses across multiple industries and sectors. Our global reach combines technical expertise, reliable repair solutions, and dedicated support to help restore equipment performance, minimize operational downtime, and keep industrial operations running efficiently—wherever our customers are located.
